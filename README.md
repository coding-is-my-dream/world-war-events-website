# Major Events of World War I (1914–1919)

An educational, chronological timeline covering key battles, political shifts, and turning points of World War I.

## 🌐 Live Website
Check out the live project here: **[nazimkalimshaikh.netlify.app](https://nazimkalimshaikh.netlify.app)**

## 📚 What's Inside
- **1914:** Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand and the outbreak of war
- **1915:** Unrestricted submarine warfare, chemical weapons, and Gallipoli
- **1916:** Battles of Verdun, Jutland, and the Somme
- **1917:** US entry into the war and the Russian Revolution
- **1918:** The Fourteen Points, major Allied offensives, and the November 11 Armistice
- **1919:** Signing of the Treaty of Versailles
